What is FireWire?

FireWire is a smart home safety device that watches your home 24 hours a day. It detects smoke, gas leaks, poisonous gas (CO), and rising heat — then immediately sounds a buzzer and sends an alert to your phone.

Even if you are away from home, FireWire will notify you through the app and by email so you can act fast.

Important: FireWire works even without internet. The buzzer and sensors always work — WiFi is only needed for phone alerts and the app dashboard.

📦

The Device & Buttons

🖥️

LCD Screen

Shows the current status — safe, warning, or alarm. Also shows WiFi status and alarm mode.

🔔

Buzzer

Continuous beep = Full alarm.
3 short beeps = Caution.
1 beep/sec = Warning.

Button Guide — press and hold each button for 2–3 seconds
1
Button 1 — Temperature & Humidity readings
2
Button 2 — Gas Level & Air Quality readings
3
Button 3 — Smoke Level readings
4
Button 4 — Carbon Monoxide (CO) readings
5
Button 5 — WiFi Hotspot Toggle
• Offline → hold to turn ON the FireWire-Setup hotspot
• Hotspot ON → hold again to turn it OFF
• Online → hold to disconnect from WiFi
• Hotspot auto-turns off after 5 minutes
The screen returns to the main status display automatically after 20 seconds of no button activity.
🖥️

Reading the Screen

The main screen shows 4 lines of information at all times:

==== FireWire ====
Status: HOME IS SAFE
Mode: Full Alarm
WiFi: Online
What each line means:
1
Line 1 — FireWire brand header (always the same)
2
Line 2 — Home Status
"Status: HOME IS SAFE" = everything is normal
"!! CAUTION !!" = something is slightly elevated, check your home
"LEAVE THE HOUSE NOW!" = full alarm, evacuate immediately
3
Line 3 — Alarm Mode
"Mode: Full Alarm" = any danger triggers alarm immediately
"Mode: Smart Alarm" = smoke alone gives a soft warning first
4
Line 4 — WiFi Status
"WiFi: Online" = connected, app alerts working
"WiFi: Offline" = no internet, alarm still works locally
"WiFi: Reconnecting.." = trying to reconnect
During startup (first 20 seconds):
==== FireWire ====
Starting up...
Ready in 15s...
WiFi: Online

The device needs 20 seconds to warm up its sensors. The alarm will not trigger during this time to prevent false alarms.

Setting Up WiFi

First time setup or changing WiFi:
1
On your phone, go to WiFi settings and connect to FireWire-Setup.
2
A setup page opens automatically. If not, open your browser and go to 192.168.4.1
3
Tap Configure WiFi, choose your home WiFi, and enter the password.
4
Tap Save. FireWire will connect and remember your WiFi from now on.
How to open the hotspot using Button 5:
5
When FireWire is offline, hold Button 5 for 2–3 seconds. The screen will show "Hotspot is ON".
5
Connect your phone to FireWire-Setup and enter your WiFi credentials.
5
Once done, hold Button 5 again to stop the hotspot, or it will stop automatically after 5 minutes.
Tip: FireWire remembers your WiFi. After setup, it will reconnect automatically every time you turn it on — as long as the WiFi is available.

Using the App

Live Monitor

See all sensor readings in real time. The colored gauges show if each sensor is safe, cautious, or in danger.

History

View all past alarms. Tap any entry to see full details — what was detected, when, and how high the readings were.

Alerts

Turn on phone notifications and email alerts. You will get notified even when the app is closed or you are away from home.

Settings

Admin only. Change alarm mode, alarm sound, and alert levels. Also where you can change the WiFi network.

What the sensor colors mean:
Green = Safe Yellow = Caution Red = Danger
Install on your phone: Open the app in your browser, tap the share button, and select "Add to Home Screen". This gives you a one-tap shortcut like a regular app.
🚨

When the Alarm Sounds

1
Stay calm. Do not panic.
2
Check the screen. It will show what triggered the alarm — smoke, gas, CO, or heat.
3
If smoke or fire — leave the house immediately. Do not stop to collect belongings. Call for help.
4
If gas leak — open windows, do not turn on lights or switches, leave the house and call for help.
5
To silence the buzzer — open the app and tap "Stop Alarm" in the Alerts tab once you are safe.
!
The alarm stops automatically once the sensor readings return to safe levels for 5 seconds. You do not need to do anything if the danger has passed.
If the buzzer keeps going for more than 30 seconds, it will automatically silence itself — but the alarm state stays visible on the screen and app until readings are safe.
⚙️

Alarm Modes

🔴

Full Alarm Mode

The default. Any sensor that detects danger triggers the full alarm immediately. Best for maximum safety.

🧠

Smart Alarm Mode

If only smoke is detected, it gives a soft warning first. Full alarm only sounds if heat also rises. Gas and CO always trigger the full alarm.

Only the Admin can change the alarm mode in the Settings tab.

📴

Using Without WiFi (Offline Mode)

FireWire works fully even without internet. The buzzer, sensors, and LCD screen all work normally. You just won't receive phone or email alerts until WiFi is restored.

What the screen shows when offline:
==== FireWire ====
Status: HOME IS SAFE
Mode: Full Alarm
WiFi: Offline
To connect to WiFi while offline:
5
Hold Button 5 for 2–3 seconds → screen shows "Hotspot is ON"
→
Connect your phone to FireWire-Setup WiFi
→
Open browser → go to 192.168.4.1 → enter your home WiFi details
→
FireWire connects and the screen shows "WiFi: Online"
The hotspot turns off automatically after 5 minutes if no one connects. Hold Button 5 again to restart it.

FireWire is designed to help protect your home. For emergencies, always call your local emergency services (911 / 117). The device is a warning tool — always follow proper fire and gas safety procedures.
