What is FireWire?

FireWire is a smart home safety device that watches your home 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. It detects smoke, gas leaks, poisonous gas (CO), and rising heat — then immediately sounds a buzzer and sends an alert to your phone.

Even if you are away from home, FireWire will notify you through this app and by email so you can act fast.

📦

The FireWire Device

🖥️

LCD Screen

Shows the current status of your home — whether it is safe, has a warning, or is in alarm. Also shows WiFi connection status and alarm mode.

🔘

5 Buttons

Press and hold each button for 2–3 seconds to see detailed sensor readings on the screen. Each button shows a different sensor.

🔔

Buzzer (Alarm Sound)

Continuous beeping = Full alarm, danger detected.
3 short beeps = Caution, check your home.
1 beep per second = Warning level.

📡

WiFi Connection

FireWire connects to your home WiFi to send alerts. If WiFi is lost, the device still works as an alarm — it just cannot send phone notifications until reconnected.

Using the App

Live Monitor

See all sensor readings in real time. Green = Safe, Yellow = Caution, Red = Danger.

History

View all past alarms. Tap any entry to see full details of what was detected.

Alerts

Turn on phone notifications and email alerts so you get notified even when the app is closed.

Settings

Admin only. Change alarm sensitivity, alarm sound, and alert levels.

Tip: Install this app on your phone's home screen for quick access. Tap the share button in your browser and select "Add to Home Screen".
🚨

What to Do When the Alarm Sounds

1
Stay calm. Do not panic.
2
Check the app. Open FireWire to see which sensor triggered the alarm.
3
If smoke or fire — leave the house immediately. Do not stop to collect belongings.
4
If gas leak — open windows, do not turn on lights or switches, leave the house and call for help.
5
To silence the alarm — tap "Stop Alarm" in the app or in the Alerts tab once you are safe.
📶

Connecting to WiFi

1
On your phone, go to WiFi settings and connect to FireWire-Setup.
2
A setup page will open automatically. If not, open your browser and go to 192.168.4.1.
3
Tap "Configure WiFi", choose your home WiFi network, and enter the password.
4
Tap Save. The device will restart and connect to your home WiFi automatically.
Note: FireWire-Setup is always available when the device is not connected to WiFi. You can use it anytime to change the WiFi network.
⚙️

Alarm Modes

🔴

Full Alarm Mode

The default mode. Any sensor that detects danger will immediately trigger the full alarm. Best for maximum safety.

🧠

Smart Alarm Mode

If only smoke is detected, it gives a soft warning instead of a full alarm. The full alarm only sounds if heat also rises. Gas and CO always trigger the full alarm.

FireWire is designed to help protect your home. For emergencies, always call your local emergency services (911 / 117).
