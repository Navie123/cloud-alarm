# FireWire Safety Guide
## Important Safety Information - Please Read!

FireWire helps detect fire and gas, but you must use it safely. Read this guide carefully.

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: What FireWire Does and Does NOT Do

### ✅ FireWire DOES:
- Detect smoke, gas, and carbon monoxide
- Sound an alarm to warn you
- Send alerts to your phone
- Work during power outages (with battery)

### ❌ FireWire Does NOT:
- Put out fires
- Stop gas leaks
- Replace fire extinguishers
- Replace calling 911 or the fire department

**Remember:** When the alarm sounds, GET OUT and call for help!

---

## Where to Install FireWire

### ✅ GOOD Locations:

**Best:** On the ceiling in the hallway or living room
- Smoke and heat rise up, so ceiling is best
- Central location can detect fires from multiple rooms

**Also Good:**
- High on the wall (within 12 inches from ceiling)
- Away from corners (air doesn't move well in corners)

### ❌ BAD Locations - Do NOT Install Here:

**Kitchen:**
- Too close to stove (less than 10 feet)
- Cooking smoke will cause false alarms

**Bathroom:**
- Steam from shower will trigger false alarms

**Garage:**
- Car exhaust will cause false alarms

**Near Air Conditioner or Fan:**
- Strong airflow prevents smoke detection

**Direct Sunlight:**
- Heat from sun can cause false alarms

**Very Hot or Cold Places:**
- Device works best at normal room temperature (0-45°C)

---

## Installation Safety

### Keep Clear Space Around Device

- **6 inches** of clear space on all sides
- Do NOT cover with curtains, decorations, or furniture
- Do NOT paint over the sensors
- Keep away from hanging plants or ceiling fans

### Mounting Safety

- Use strong screws or mounting tape
- Make sure it won't fall down
- Keep out of reach of young children
- Install where you can easily reach the buttons

---

## Electrical Safety

### Power Supply

**Using USB Charger:**
- Use only phone chargers (5V)
- Do NOT use fast chargers or laptop chargers
- Plug into a safe outlet, not an extension cord if possible

**Using Battery:**
- Device can run on battery for 4-6 hours
- Charge regularly to keep battery ready

### Wiring Safety

- Do NOT cut or modify any wires
- If wires are damaged, stop using and get help
- Keep wires away from water
- Do NOT use if you see exposed wires

---

## Battery Safety

### ⚠️ VERY IMPORTANT - Battery Rules

**DO:**
- ✅ Use only genuine 18650 batteries (Samsung, Panasonic, LG)
- ✅ Buy from trusted electronics stores
- ✅ Check batteries for damage before installing
- ✅ Charge at room temperature
- ✅ Unplug when fully charged
- ✅ Recycle old batteries properly

**DO NOT:**
- ❌ Use cheap or unknown brand batteries
- ❌ Use damaged, dented, or swollen batteries
- ❌ Let metal objects touch battery terminals
- ❌ Throw batteries in regular trash
- ❌ Charge overnight unattended
- ❌ Expose batteries to heat or fire
- ❌ Take batteries apart

### Warning Signs - Stop Using Immediately If:

- Battery feels very hot
- Battery smells strange
- Battery is swollen or leaking
- Device makes strange sounds
- You see smoke from the device

**If this happens:** Unplug immediately, move to a safe place, do NOT use water on battery fires!

## Fire Safety

### When the Alarm Sounds

**Step 1: Check for Fire**
- Look for smoke or flames
- Smell for burning
- Feel doors for heat (don't open hot doors!)

**Step 2: If You See Fire**
1. GET EVERYONE OUT immediately
2. Do NOT try to fight large fires
3. Close doors behind you
4. Call fire department: **8426-0219** (Bureau of Fire Protection)
5. Do NOT go back inside

**Step 3: If No Fire Found**
- Check if you're cooking (smoke from cooking)
- Check for gas smell
- Open windows for fresh air
- Press any button to silence alarm

### Have a Fire Escape Plan

- Know 2 ways out of every room
- Choose a meeting place outside
- Practice with your family
- Make sure children know what to do

### Keep Fire Extinguishers

- Have at least one fire extinguisher at home
- Keep in kitchen and near bedrooms
- Learn how to use it
- Check expiration date yearly

## Carbon Monoxide (CO) Safety

### What is Carbon Monoxide?

- Invisible, odorless gas
- Comes from burning fuel (gas stoves, heaters, cars)
- Can make you sick or kill you
- FireWire detects it and warns you

### If CO Alarm Sounds

**Symptoms of CO Poisoning:**
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Nausea
- Confusion
- Feeling very tired

**What to Do:**
1. Open all windows and doors immediately
2. Turn off gas stoves and heaters
3. Get everyone outside to fresh air
4. Call for help if anyone feels sick
5. Do NOT go back inside until safe

---

## Gas Leak Safety

### If You Smell Gas

**Gas smells like rotten eggs**

**What to Do:**
1. Do NOT turn lights on or off
2. Do NOT use phone inside the house
3. Do NOT light matches or lighters
4. Open windows and doors
5. Turn off gas tank if safe to do so
6. Get everyone out
7. Call gas company or fire department from outside

**What NOT to Do:**
- Do NOT look for the leak yourself
- Do NOT try to fix it yourself
- Do NOT use electrical switches

---

## Child Safety

### Keep Children Safe

- Install device high where children can't reach
- Teach children what the alarm sounds like
- Practice fire drills with children
- Explain that FireWire is not a toy
- Do NOT let children play with buttons
- Teach children to tell adults if alarm sounds

### Teach Children

**When alarm sounds:**
1. Tell an adult immediately
2. If adult says to leave, go to meeting place outside
3. Do NOT hide
4. Do NOT go back for toys or pets
5. Stay low if there's smoke (crawl under smoke)

---

## Daily Safety Tips

### Prevent Fires at Home

- Don't leave cooking unattended
- Keep matches and lighters away from children
- Don't overload electrical outlets
- Check electrical cords for damage
- Don't smoke in bed
- Keep flammable items away from stoves and heaters
- Turn off appliances when not in use

### Prevent Gas Leaks

- Check gas hose connections regularly
- Replace old or cracked gas hoses
- Turn off gas tank when not cooking
- Have gas appliances checked yearly
- Ventilate when using gas stoves

### Prevent CO Poisoning

- Never use gas stoves for heating
- Don't run cars in closed garages
- Have chimneys cleaned yearly
- Ventilate rooms when using heaters

## If Device is Damaged

### Stop Using If:

- Device fell and cracked
- Water got inside
- Wires are exposed or cut
- Smoke or strange smell from device
- Device very hot to touch
- Screen completely dark and won't turn on

**What to do:** Unplug immediately and contact support for repair or replacement.
