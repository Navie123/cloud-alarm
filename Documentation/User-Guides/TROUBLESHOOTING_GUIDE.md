# FireWire Troubleshooting Guide
## Quick Fixes for Common Problems

Having problems with your FireWire? This guide will help you fix most issues yourself.

---

## 🔍 How to Use This Guide

1. Find your problem in the list below
2. Try the solutions in order (easiest first)
3. If nothing works, see "Getting Help" at the bottom

---

## Screen Problems

### Problem: Screen is Completely Black

**Possible Causes:**
- Device is off
- Battery is dead
- Screen brightness too low

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Check if the power switch is ON
2. Plug in the USB charger
3. Wait 1 minute and check again

**If Still Black:**
1. Look at the back of the screen
2. Find the small blue knob
3. Turn it slowly to the right (clockwise)
4. Screen should get brighter

**Still Not Working?**
- Battery might be completely dead - charge for 30 minutes then try again
- Contact support if still black after charging

---

### Problem: Screen Shows Weird Characters or Blocks

**What This Means:** Screen contrast is wrong

**Solution:**
1. Find the small blue knob on the back of the screen
2. Turn it slowly left and right until you see clear text
3. Stop when text looks normal

---

### Problem: Screen is Too Dim or Too Bright

**Solution:**
1. Find the small blue knob on the back of the screen
2. Turn right = brighter
3. Turn left = darker
4. Adjust until comfortable to read

---

## WiFi Connection Problems

### Problem: Can't Connect to WiFi

**Possible Causes:**
- Wrong password
- Too far from router
- Router is off

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Check if other devices can connect to WiFi (phone, laptop)
2. If they can't, restart your WiFi router:
   - Unplug router
   - Wait 30 seconds
   - Plug back in
   - Wait 2 minutes

**If Router is Working:**
1. Press and hold Button 5 for 2 seconds
2. Wait for "FireWire-Setup" to appear on screen
3. Connect your phone to "FireWire-Setup" WiFi
4. Enter your WiFi password again (check for typos!)
5. Click Save

**Common Mistakes:**
- Typing password wrong (check CAPITAL letters)
- Connecting to 5GHz WiFi (FireWire only works with 2.4GHz)
- WiFi name has special characters

**How to Check WiFi Type:**
- Look at your router settings
- 2.4GHz WiFi usually has longer range
- If your WiFi name ends in "-5G" or "5GHz", that's the wrong one

---

### Problem: Device Says "Offline" on Website

**What This Means:** Device is not sending data to the internet

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Check the screen - does it show "WiFi: Connected"?
2. If NO, see "Can't Connect to WiFi" above
3. If YES, continue below

**If WiFi Shows Connected:**
1. Restart the device:
   - Turn OFF
   - Wait 10 seconds
   - Turn ON
   - Wait 1 minute
2. Refresh the website
3. Check if it says "Online" now

**Still Offline?**
- Your internet might be down (not just WiFi)
- Check if you can browse websites on your phone
- If internet is down, device will work locally but not online

## Sensor Problems

### Problem: Alarm Keeps Going Off (False Alarms)

**Possible Causes:**
- Cooking smoke
- Dust on sensors
- Device too close to kitchen
- Sensors need calibration

**Solutions:**

**If Cooking:**
1. Open windows
2. Turn on exhaust fan
3. Press any button to silence alarm
4. Consider enabling "Smart Alarm Mode":
   - Go to website
   - Log in as admin
   - Click Settings
   - Turn ON "Smart Alarm Mode"
   - This reduces cooking false alarms

**If Not Cooking:**
1. Clean the sensors (see Maintenance Guide)
2. Calibrate the sensors:
   - Take device outside or to fresh air
   - Go to website → Settings
   - Click "Calibrate Sensors"
   - Wait 30 seconds

**If Still Getting False Alarms:**
- Device might be too close to kitchen
- Move it farther away (at least 10 feet from stove)

---

### Problem: Alarm Doesn't Sound When Testing

**What This Means:** Sensors might not be working

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Check if device just turned on (needs 3 minutes to warm up)
2. Wait 3 minutes after turning on
3. Try test again

**If Still Not Working:**
1. Check the screen - do numbers change when you blow on it?
2. If numbers don't change, sensors might be broken
3. Contact support for repair

**Test the Buzzer:**
1. Go to website
2. Click "Test Alarm" button
3. If buzzer sounds, sensors are the problem
4. If buzzer doesn't sound, buzzer is broken

---

### Problem: Sensor Numbers Look Wrong

**Examples:**
- Temperature shows 0°C or 99°C
- Gas shows 0% all the time
- Smoke shows 100% all the time

**What This Means:** Sensor connection problem or sensor broken

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Restart the device (turn OFF, wait 10 seconds, turn ON)
2. Wait 3 minutes for sensors to warm up
3. Check numbers again

**If Still Wrong:**
1. Calibrate sensors (see Maintenance Guide)
2. If calibration doesn't help, sensor might be broken
3. Contact support

---

## Battery Problems

### Problem: Battery Dies Too Fast (Less Than 2 Hours)

**Possible Causes:**
- Old battery
- Battery not fully charged
- Cheap/fake battery

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Charge battery fully (until green light)
2. This takes 5-6 hours
3. Test how long it lasts

**If Still Dies Fast:**
1. Check battery age (written on battery)
2. If more than 3 years old, replace battery
3. If new battery, might be fake - buy from trusted store

**How to Know Battery is Bad:**
- Swollen or puffy
- Gets very hot when charging
- Won't charge to full (stays below 3.7V)
- Only lasts 1-2 hours

---

### Problem: Battery Won't Charge

**Possible Causes:**
- Charger not plugged in properly
- Bad USB cable
- Charging port dirty

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Check if charger is plugged into wall
2. Check if USB cable is fully inserted
3. Try a different USB cable
4. Try a different wall charger

**Check the Lights:**
- Red light = charging (good!)
- Green light = fully charged (good!)
- No light = not charging (problem!)

**If No Light:**
1. Look at charging port - is it dirty?
2. Gently clean with dry toothbrush
3. Try charging again

**Still Not Charging?**
- Charging port might be broken
- Contact support for repair

## Website/App Problems

### Problem: Can't Open Website

**Website:** https://cloud-alarm.onrender.com

**Possible Causes:**
- No internet on your phone/computer
- Website is loading (takes 30-60 seconds first time)

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Check if you have internet (try opening Google)
2. If no internet, connect to WiFi or mobile data
3. Try website again

**If You Have Internet:**
1. Wait 60 seconds (website might be "waking up")
2. Refresh the page
3. Try again

**If Still Not Loading:**
- Clear your browser cache:
  - Phone: Settings → Browser → Clear Data
  - Computer: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Clear Cache
- Try again

---

### Problem: Can't Log In

**Possible Causes:**
- Wrong password
- Wrong code
- Code expired

**Solutions:**

**For Admin Login:**
1. Check your email for the 6-digit code
2. Code expires after 10 minutes - request new one if old
3. Check spam folder if no email
4. Make sure you're typing PIN correctly (numbers only)

**For Family Member Login:**
1. Check Home Password (ask admin if forgot)
2. Check Family Code (6 digits, ask admin if forgot)
3. Make sure no extra spaces when typing

**Forgot Password?**
- Only admin can reset
- Admin must log in and change password in Settings

---

### Problem: Not Getting Alarm Notifications on Phone

**Possible Causes:**
- Notifications not enabled
- Phone settings blocking notifications

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Open website on your phone
2. Log in
3. Click Settings
4. Click "Enable Notifications"
5. Click "Allow" when phone asks

**If Still Not Getting Notifications:**

**On Android:**
1. Go to Phone Settings
2. Apps → Browser (Chrome/Firefox)
3. Notifications → Turn ON
4. Try test notification on website

**On iPhone:**
- iPhone has limited support for web notifications
- Notifications might not work when app is closed
- Use Android phone or computer for reliable notifications

## Button Problems

### Problem: Buttons Don't Work

**Possible Causes:**
- Buttons stuck
- Wiring loose

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Press buttons harder
2. Press and hold for 1 second
3. Try each button one at a time

**If Some Buttons Work:**
- Stuck button might be the problem
- Press stuck button multiple times quickly
- If still stuck, contact support

**If No Buttons Work:**
- Wiring might be loose
- Contact support for repair

---

### Problem: Screen Doesn't Change When Button Pressed

**What This Means:** Display timeout or button not registering

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Press button and hold for 1 full second
2. Release and check screen
3. Screen should change immediately

**If Screen Changes Then Goes Back:**
- This is normal!
- Screen returns to main view after 15 seconds
- Press button again to see that screen

---

## Buzzer Problems

### Problem: Buzzer Too Loud

**Solution:**
- Buzzer volume cannot be adjusted
- This is for safety (must be loud to wake you up)
- If too loud, move device farther from bedrooms

---

### Problem: Buzzer Doesn't Stop

**Possible Causes:**
- Alarm condition still present
- Button not pressed properly

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Press ANY button firmly
2. Buzzer should stop
3. Check screen for alarm cause

**If Buzzer Continues:**
1. Open windows to clear smoke/gas
2. Wait 1 minute
3. Buzzer should stop when air clears

**If Still Buzzing:**
1. Turn device OFF
2. Open all windows
3. Check for fire or gas leak
4. Turn device back ON after 5 minutes

---

## Other Problems

### Problem: Device Restarts By Itself

**Possible Causes:**
- Low battery
- Power problem
- Software issue

**Solutions:**

**Try This First:**
1. Check battery level
2. If low, charge immediately
3. Keep plugged in while using

**If Battery is Full:**
1. Restart device properly:
   - Turn OFF
   - Wait 30 seconds
   - Turn ON
2. If keeps restarting, contact support

---

### Problem: Device Gets Very Hot

**What This Means:** This is NOT normal

**What to Do:**
1. Turn OFF immediately
2. Unplug charger
3. Let it cool down for 30 minutes
4. Do NOT use until checked by support
5. Contact support - might be dangerous

---

### Problem: Strange Smell from Device

**What This Means:** Possible electrical problem or battery issue

**What to Do:**
1. Turn OFF immediately
2. Unplug charger
3. Move to safe, open area
4. Do NOT use
5. Contact support immediately

## Prevention Tips

### To Avoid Problems:

**Daily:**
- Keep device powered on
- Keep battery charged above 50%

**Weekly:**
- Check that device is working
- Look for any damage

**Monthly:**
- Clean sensors
- Calibrate sensors
- Test alarm

**Every 3 Months:**
- Check all parts
- Test battery life
- Update WiFi password if changed
