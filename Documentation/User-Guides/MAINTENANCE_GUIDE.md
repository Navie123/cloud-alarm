# FireWire Maintenance Guide
## Keeping Your Fire Alarm System Working Properly

This guide shows you how to take care of your FireWire system. Follow these simple steps to keep your family safe.

---

## Monthly Tasks (Do Once a Month)

### 1. Clean the Sensors

**Why:** Dust can block the sensors and cause false alarms or prevent the alarm from working.

**How to do it:**
1. Turn off the device
2. Use a soft, dry cloth to gently wipe the outside
3. Do NOT use water or cleaning spray on the sensors
4. Turn the device back on

**Time needed:** 2 minutes

---

### 2. Check the Sensors (Calibration)

**Why:** Sensors need to be "reset" in clean air so they can detect smoke and gas correctly.

**How to do it:**
1. Take the device outside or to a room with fresh air (open windows)
2. Wait 5 minutes
3. Open the website on your phone: https://cloud-alarm.onrender.com
4. Log in as admin
5. Click "Settings"
6. Click "Calibrate Sensors"
7. Wait 30 seconds
8. Done! The numbers should show close to zero

**Time needed:** 10 minutes

**When to do it:** Once a month, or if you notice false alarms

---

### 3. Check the Battery

**Why:** The battery keeps the alarm working during power outages.

**What to check:**
- Look at the screen or website - battery should show 3.7V or higher
- If it shows "Low Battery" - charge it soon
- If the device turns off by itself - battery needs charging

**How to charge:**
1. Plug a phone charger cable (micro-USB) into the device
2. Red light = charging
3. Green light = fully charged
4. Takes 5-6 hours to fully charge

**Time needed:** 2 minutes to check, 5-6 hours to charge

---

## Every 3 Months (4 Times a Year)

### 1. Test the Alarm

**Why:** Make sure the buzzer works and everyone knows what it sounds like.

**How to do it:**
1. Tell everyone in the house you're testing the alarm
2. Light a match or incense near the device (not too close!)
3. Wait 10-20 seconds
4. The buzzer should sound and the screen should show "ALARM"
5. Press any button to silence it
6. Open windows to clear the smoke

**Time needed:** 5 minutes

---

### 2. Check All Parts

**What to check:**
- [ ] Screen is bright and easy to read
- [ ] All 5 buttons work when pressed
- [ ] Buzzer is loud and clear
- [ ] No cracks or damage on the case
- [ ] Wires are not loose or exposed

**If something is broken:** Contact support or bring to a repair shop

**Time needed:** 5 minutes

---

## Battery Care

### When to Replace Batteries

Replace the batteries if:
- Device only works for 1-2 hours on battery (should last 4-6 hours)
- Battery won't charge to full (stays below 3.7V)
- Battery looks swollen or damaged
- Battery is more than 3 years old

### How to Replace Batteries

**What you need:**
- 2 new 18650 batteries (buy from electronics store)
- Small screwdriver

**Steps:**
1. Turn off the device
2. Unplug the charging cable
3. Remove the screws on the battery cover
4. Take out the old batteries
5. Look at the + and - marks inside
6. Put in new batteries matching the + and - marks
7. Put the cover back and tighten screws
8. Turn on the device

**Important:** Use only genuine 18650 batteries from Samsung, Panasonic, or LG. Do NOT use cheap batteries - they can be dangerous!

**Time needed:** 10 minutes

---

## Screen Care

### If Screen is Too Dark or Too Bright

There's a small blue knob on the back of the screen:
- Turn it slowly to the right = brighter
- Turn it slowly to the left = darker

### If Screen is Dirty

1. Turn off the device
2. Use a soft cloth (like for eyeglasses)
3. Slightly dampen with water (not wet!)
4. Gently wipe the screen
5. Let it dry completely
6. Turn on the device

**Do NOT use:** Alcohol, window cleaner, or paper towels

---

## WiFi Connection

### If WiFi Password Changes

1. Press and hold Button 5 (the last button) for 2 seconds
2. Screen will show "FireWire-Setup"
3. On your phone, connect to "FireWire-Setup" WiFi
4. A page will open automatically
5. Choose your home WiFi from the list
6. Type in the new password
7. Click "Save"
8. Device will restart and connect

**Time needed:** 5 minutes
